About Theorrem

We build AI agents around real business workflows.

Theorrem helps businesses move from AI curiosity to practical automation. We design controlled AI agents that work with your existing tools, your processes, and your team’s approval rules.

Built for outcomes, not demos.

Most teams do not need another generic chatbot. They need repeatable workflows handled better: lead qualification, customer replies, CRM updates, reports, approvals, follow-ups, and operational handoffs.

Our approach starts with one high-friction process, maps how your team already works, and then builds an AI agent around that process with the right controls.
