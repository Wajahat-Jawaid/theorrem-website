Privacy policy

Theorrem collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries, understand your business requirements, and provide our services. This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, website, and details you voluntarily share through forms, email, calls, or meetings.

We use this information to communicate with you, evaluate potential workflows, prepare proposals, provide support, improve our website, and maintain business records. We do not sell your personal information.

If we connect to business tools such as CRM systems, helpdesks, spreadsheets, messaging platforms, databases, or internal APIs, we use that access only for the workflow agreed with you. Access permissions are scoped to the selected use case whenever possible.

Our website may use basic analytics or similar tools to understand traffic and improve user experience. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Security policy

Theorrem designs AI workflows with practical controls from the start. Depending on the workflow, this can include access limits, audit logs, approval gates, confidence thresholds, restricted tool access, monitoring, and human review for sensitive actions.

We aim to follow least-privilege access. Agents should only access the tools, data, and actions required for the approved workflow. High-impact actions such as refunds, pricing changes, customer-sensitive decisions, or production updates can be routed through human approval before execution.

We recommend that every production workflow includes a testing phase using real examples, edge cases, and quality checks before going live. We also encourage clear ownership, escalation paths, and documented rollback processes.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe you have found a security issue related to Theorrem, please contact us at info@theorrem.com with enough detail for us to investigate.
